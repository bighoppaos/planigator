This page shows the buttons on the planner and what each one does. The pictures are the same buttons, drawn here so you can match them. They are not a live map, and they are not a saved trip.

Planigator plans a truck trip in the browser, then navigates that route on the same page. The clocks are a planning aid. They are not legal advice and they are not a substitute for your ELD.

Plan a trip

Load an example trip sits under the front picture. Then the planner is seven steps, in order.

Step 1. Set speed, hours, and when you leave

Governed speed65 Leave now Leave at6:00 AM Start anytime Day start6:00 AM End anytime Day end8:00 PM Military time Kilometers Hours I’ll drive out of the 1111 Hours into driving before 30-minute break8
  • Governed speed starts on at 65. Tap the number to pick 55 through 75. Tap Governed speed to turn the cap off. Changing it does not move the clocks. A note under the button says why you may want to Recalculate: only HERE© can figure the drive hours for the new speed. Fast mode may also pick different roads.
  • Leave now uses the current time. Leave at is the clock you pick. The numbers you type stay the numbers you typed.
  • Day start is 6:00 AM and Day end is 8:00 PM until you change them. Driving stops at the day end. Start anytime and End anytime turn those clocks off.
  • Military time shows 24-hour clocks on the stamps, such as 18:00 instead of 6:00 PM. Kilometers shows distance in kilometers.
  • Hours I’ll drive out of the 11 starts at 11. You can set it from 1 to 11. Hours into driving before the 30-minute break starts at 8.
  • After a plan exists, changing Leave now, Leave at, the day clocks, the 11, or the 30 rebuilds the clocks. Leave now also moves every minute. That does not use a credit, and it does not ask HERE© for a new route.

Step 2. Choose where the trip starts

Start from my location Start from an address

Green means that choice is on.

Start from my location uses the phone or computer location. The browser asks first. That lookup is free. Start from an address is a normal stop: you type it, then look it up, and that lookup is 1 credit. If you press Calculate before choosing one of these, the page moves up to this step.

Step 3. Sign in

Sign in with Google. A new account gets 40 credits. After you sign in, Saved trips shows under this step and those trips stay with that account. Signed out, this page does not show that account’s trip or location, and it does not keep them in this browser. Clear trip sits at the top of Add each stop, and only after an address is typed.

Step 4. Add each stop

Each stop is a card. See Stops for every button on the card. Trip name and Save sit at the end of this step. Save keeps the trip on your account when you are signed in. Signed out, it stays on this device until you sign in.

Step 5. Calculate the truck route

Fast mode Short mode Calculate · 1 credit Recalculate from Stop 1 · 1 credit

Fast mode picks the least time. Short mode picks the least distance. The green one is the one that will be used. Fast mode is the start.

Calculate asks HERE© for a truck route: a 53-ft trailer, 80,000 lb, 13 ft 6 in high. Truck only. Not car, bike, or walk. Each HERE answer is 1 credit. A leg is the drive from one stop to the next. The start is not a leg. Short mode can ask a second time, and that answer is another credit.

After a route exists, a new stop added at the end calculates only from the last stop that already has miles. The button then says Recalculate from that stop, and only the new leg uses a credit. A stop inserted in the middle recalculates the trip, and each leg that has to be routed uses a credit. Stops you have already reached are skipped.

Step 6. The plan

Leave by, Arrive by, Driving, HOS on this path, and total trip time sit at the top. Under those, each drive says Toward and the stop name. The 30-minute breaks, Off-duty/Sleeper Berth, leeway, and finish chips follow. A done stop shows the green check with a smile in this list, and the rest of the plan runs without that stop. On the last stop, Leeway runs to the window close or be-there-by. Share trip link and Add Planigator to your home screen sit at the end of this step. Share trip link puts the plan in the address bar so you can send it. When a signed-in driver opens that link, the whole trip is added to his account and shows under Saved trips. Add this trip to my account does the same thing for the trip on the page.

Step 7. Navigate

The map and auto zooming directions are here, with a little space between them. Before you start navigation, the map is in Trip zoom. Start navigation and End navigation sit just under the directions. See Map buttons and Navigate.

One stop is 2 credits: 1 to look up the address, and 1 to calculate the leg. Ten stops are 20.

  • Looking up a start address adds 1 credit. A one-stop trip is then 3.
  • Use this spot is 1 credit. It asks HERE for the address. If HERE answers, that credit stays, even when no address comes back. The point stays. Calculate is still 1 more credit for that leg.
  • Search on the map is 1 credit and takes the place of Look up this address. If HERE answers and nothing matches, that credit stays. Calculate is still 1 credit for the leg.
  • A credit comes back only when HERE does not answer. An answer that finds nothing still uses the credit.
  • Navigation, the compass, zoom, elevation, and wind do not use credits. Moving the clocks when you change a time, or when Leave now ticks forward, does not use a credit.
  • Finding the next Love's, Walmart, Cat scale, or truck stop does not use a credit. Adding a place onto the route is 1 credit for each truck route HERE answers.

Google sign-in gives 40 credits. The first time a debit or credit card is saved, that account gets 40 more, once. A prepaid card does not get that extra 40. Removing the card and adding another one does not give another 40. The saved card is not charged when the free credits run out. Extra packs are 124 credits for $1.49, and that button shows only when a card is already on file.

Stops

Paste search/choose from map Look up this address · 1 credit Anytime Window Add a stop before Stop 1 Add a stop after Stop 1
  • The stop name is at most 8 characters.
  • Paste fills the address. If the pasted text has times, those can fill too. You still look up the address. Lookup is 1 credit. Tap one of the suggestions. Choosing the suggestion does not cost another credit.
  • search/choose from map opens a map. Search · 1 credit finds places and drops red pins. Love's, Walmart, Cat scale, and Swift terminals search the part of the map you are looking at and do not use a credit. Truck stop on that map uses the saved list and does not use a credit. Each of those pins shows how far the place is from the current location. Tap a pin to use it, or press Use this address after a search. Or long-press the map and then press Use this spot. Signed in, Use this spot is 1 credit. If HERE answers, that credit stays.
  • Anytime means no appointment clock. Window shows Opens and Closes. Without Window, the clock is Be there by.
  • The up and down arrows move the stop. The minus removes it. Tap the minus again when it says Remove. That stop’s directions come off the list, including while navigation is running.
  • Add a stop before the first one sits above that card. Add a stop after this one sits under the card, so you can still add a stop after one you have already reached.

Times, delays, and a finished stop

After Calculate, the plan lists Leave by, Arrive by, Driving, HOS on this path, and total trip time at the top. Under that, each drive says Toward and the stop name. Each 30-minute break and each Off-duty/Sleeper Berth follows. Leeway chips say Leeway. On the last stop, that spare time runs to the window close or be-there-by.

Changing Leave now, Leave at, a day clock, the 11, the 30, a stop time, or a delay rebuilds those times. Leave now moves again every minute, including the leeway that is waiting on the current minute. Removing a stop does not stop that. None of that asks HERE© for a new road. Governed speed does not. The clocks stay on the last HERE© hours until you Calculate.

On a driving line you can add Possible delay time, in minutes. Those minutes come out of the hours you can drive that day, and they count on the clock toward the 30-minute break. Drive time plus possible delay before Off-duty cannot go past the hours you chose out of the 11. A delay on a drive before a 30 shortens that chip’s hours so the break still lands at the hours into driving you chose. Later drives move instead, so a stack of delays cannot push you past that break mark. If those drives would pass the end of the day, they move to after the reset. Delay that does not fit before the day ends comes out of the Off-duty/Sleeper Berth. Off-duty never goes under 10 hours. Slack above 10 can get shorter. When the rest is already at 10 hours, more delay pushes the morning start later instead of shortening the rest. When that day is used up, a 10-hour reset is added. That reset clears the clock, and the next drive starts when the reset ends. A delay on the last drive moves that arrival. Time left before the window absorbs it. If the arrival passes the be-there-by, that drive is late. Changing Possible delay time does not auto-zoom the map.

On the finish chip, Possible delay time is a live load. You are not driving while they load you, so those minutes come out of the 14-hour day only. They do not come out of drive time. The next drive leaves after that timer. When the 14 is used up, a 10-hour reset is added and the clock clears. Load time that did not fit does not follow. Neither timer uses a credit or asks HERE© for a new road.

The map does not ask you to switch stops. When you pick another stop yourself, it asks if the stop you were on is done. Yes marks that stop done and starts the one you picked. No stays on the stop you were on. A green check with a smile covers a stop that is done. The word Done is not on the card. Recalculate skips a stop you marked done. You can still add a stop after it. The minus on that card still removes it. Tap it, then tap Remove.

Map buttons

Before full screen, the right side of the map has these. The compass is always there. Elevation and wind are not.

Not full screen. Top to bottom: Full screen, compass, Trip zoom (default before navigation), Recalculate. Follow me appears after Start navigation.

Full screen while you are navigating adds Exit, elevation, wind, Stop, Detour, and the place buttons on the left. The zoom button cycles. This picture is Turn zoom, which is how navigation starts.

Full screen, navigating, on an eastbound road. North stays at the top of the compass. The arrow points at E, the way the road goes. Elevation here is 1,125 feet and going down. Wind is 8 mph. A green ring means that button is on. Follow me is separate from Turn zoom.

  • Full screen fills the page with the map. Exit leaves full screen. Exit is only there in full screen.
  • Elevation shows only in full screen. Before the phone reports a height, the button says Elev and a dash. Then it shows feet from the phone GPS. Until that number changes, the second line stays a dash. One foot lower says Down. One foot higher says Up. It is not sent to Planigator.
  • Wind shows only in full screen. Before it loads, the button says Wind and a dash. Then it shows the speed and mph. The number comes from a weather service, not from Planigator, and it is not sent to Planigator. It refreshes about every 10 minutes or about 5 miles, when you enter full screen, and when you tap the wind button.
  • Street map and Satellite are the same button. Tap it to switch. The green ring is the map you are on. In a tunnel the switch waits, then happens when the phone has a signal again.
  • Zoom in and Zoom out change how close the map is.
  • Recalculate on the map needs location. It asks HERE© for a new truck route from where you are to the next stop that is not done. That is 1 credit. It does not offer a U-turn. Stops you already passed are left behind.
  • Follow me appears after Start navigation. It is only the close view on you. It is not Turn zoom. The green ring means the map is following you. Dragging the map turns that off. Tap Follow me to follow again.

Navigate

Start navigation End navigation Light mode Dark mode

Start navigation stays on this page. The browser asks before it uses location, compass, or motion. Those readings stay on the phone. They are not saved and they are not sent to Planigator. The button then says Navigation in progress. End navigation stops it. The zoom button goes back to Trip zoom. An hour of driving does not sign you out. Navigation counts as using the site.

A stop you mark done stays done. It stays done after a refresh, after you leave the app, after a new build, and after End navigation. The done mark is saved with the trip, and with your account when you are signed in, so it is still there when you open that trip again. Opening a different trip, Clear trip, or Load an example trip does not clear it. Each trip keeps its own done marks. Navigation stays on the stop you are driving toward until you pick the next stop and say the one you were on is done. It does not move on when you get to the stop, when you drive onto the road to another stop, or after a refresh.

The stop you are driving toward is a blue line. The other stops stay green. The lighter blue is the part of that stop still ahead of you. The arrival chip stays on one line: arrival time, miles left, and hours and minutes left.

On the map on the page, the small chip at the bottom, next to the town name, shows only the drive time left to the stop you are driving toward, like 2 hr 32 min left. It has no miles and no arrival time, and it never adds delay time. It counts down as you drive, and it shows the same hours and minutes as the arrival chip in full screen. In full screen, that small chip still shows the drive time left in the whole trip.

Before navigation, the map is in Trip zoom. Navigation starts in Turn zoom. Each tap of the zoom button moves to the next view:

Tripzoom Leftzoom Turnzoom Stopzoom
  • Trip zoom shows the whole route, north up, clear of the directions and the side buttons. This is the default before you start navigation, and again after End navigation.
  • Left zoom shows what is left, north up.
  • Turn zoom is the default once navigation is running. You stay at the bottom of the map, at Recalculate height. In full screen that is just above the directions. The map fits the whole road from you to the next turn, even when that turn is a long highway run many miles ahead. That road runs up the screen from you, including when it curves, and the whole stretch stays on the map between the side buttons and above the directions. In full screen the next turn stays about an inch from the top of the screen, below the notch. The map only shows it lower when that is the only way to keep all of the road to it on the screen, or in the last few hundred feet before the turn. The map tightens as the turn gets closer. You and the turn stay clear of the buttons, the ETA chip, and the directions. After you pass a turn, you stay low on the map. The next turn moves to the top of the view once it is under a mile away, or once you have driven a mile, whichever comes first. The map turns so that stretch points up unless north is locked. It keeps that lock while Planigator is on screen, including after a notification. You do not have to leave the app and come back for the map to catch up. In landscape, each side has two rails of three buttons beside the directions. Exit sits at the top.
  • Stop zoom shows you and the next stop, north up.
  • Follow me is a separate button. It is only the close view on you. It does not turn into Turn zoom.

While you are driving, the directions show the step you are on, the one before it, and the one after it. The header shows the stop you are driving toward, to the right of the word directions. A long name stays on one line and is cut off. It changes only when you pick the next stop and say the one you were on is done. Each line names the next move. The miles on the step you are on count down from where you are, to the tenth of a mile, and they come first. The next step stays at the miles it will be when you reach it. It starts counting down once that step is the current one. A turn you already passed does not keep a mileage. The voice uses the step you are on. Stop on the left side of the map opens the stops in a column. Tap one to start toward that stop. If that stop is not the one you were on, the map asks if the stop you were on is done. Press Stop again to close the column. Detour opens Swift terminals, Love's, Walmart, a truck stop, and Cat scale the same way. Tapping a direction holds that line still for a few seconds. Voice is with Start navigation and End navigation. The arrows step through Phone, US, Clear, Ann, Cal, Scot, and North. Phone is the voice on the phone and pauses the song. The others talk when you tap them and keep the song playing. It also says the next move at 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, and 0.5 miles. A whole mile is said as 5 miles, not 5.0. The screen may say Tpke. The voice says Turnpike. A dash in a route number, such as US-220A, is not spoken. The city and state come from the phone's location. That lookup stays on the phone. It does not ask HERE and it does not use a credit.

Compass

Left: driving east on a north-up map. N is at the top. The arrow points at E. Right: north is locked. The ring is green and the arrow is an outline.

N on the compass is north on the map. The arrow is the way you are driving, not a second north. On an eastbound road with north up, N stays at the top and the arrow points at E.

Tap the compass to lock the map to true north. Tap it again to follow the road. Locking north does not change Trip, Left, Turn, or Stop zoom. Trip, Left, and Stop stay north up either way. Turn zoom follows the road until you lock north.

Love's, Walmart, truck stops, Cat scales

Next truck stop Next Cat scale Next Love's Next Walmart Add as next stop Add and recalculate

The four Next buttons sit under the directions. They do nothing until Start navigation. Love's, Walmart, and Cat scale are searched from lists stored with this site, in this browser, and do not use a credit. Love's, Walmart, and truck stop locations are from OpenStreetMap contributors. Cat scale locations are from the public CAT Scale list. A brand-new store can be missing until that list is updated. None of those lists use a credit. The place has to be within about 5 miles of the route ahead.

Detour also has Swift terminals. It does not use a credit. Every terminal within about 5 miles of the route ahead is pinned. Move the map and Search here shows the terminals in that view, including ones off the route.

Next Love's, Next Walmart, and Next Cat scale show up to five places ahead. The nearest one is selected. Tap another line, or another pin, to choose it. On the map each pin shows the name, town, miles ahead, and miles off the route. Add and recalculate is on the pin you chose. Clear takes the pins off the map and returns to Turn zoom. After Love's, Walmart, Cat scale, or a truck stop, Search here looks in the part of the map you are looking at, including places that are not on the route. On the full-screen map, Search here and Clear sit together at the top after you press one of those buttons. Clear stays there after you choose a stop. Search here for a truck stop uses the saved list and does not use a credit. Add as next stop uses the one you chose. Next truck stop still shows one.

Add as next stop puts that place on the stop list. You still Calculate to put it on the road.

In full screen the same four places are round buttons on the left. After one is selected, Add and recalculate on that pin routes it in as the next stop and returns the map to Turn zoom. That uses 2 credits: from where you are to that place, then from that place to the stop you were driving toward. You need 2 credits left. The new stop is Anytime. Clear removes the pins if you do not want them.

Share, dark mode, and home screen

  • Share trip link sits at the end of the plan. It puts this plan in the address bar.
  • Light mode and Dark mode appear after directions exist. The button names the mode you are in. It starts in light mode, so the button says Light mode. Tap it to switch. The map stays up.
  • Add Planigator to your home screen sits at the end of the plan, when the phone or browser can install the page.
  • If you need more credits, buy 124 credits for $1.49 appears when a card is already saved.
