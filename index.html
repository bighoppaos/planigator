Support

This is the public support page for the SWIFF PLANIGATOR iPhone app. We do not have user accounts. Trips live on your phone. Leftover credits follow the same Apple ID through iCloud.

Billing and credits

Credit packs are sold through Apple. To refund or manage a purchase:

  • On iPhone: Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → Purchase History, or
  • reportaproblem.apple.com

Used credits stay used. Leftover credits follow this Apple ID through iCloud, so a new phone signed into the same account gets the leftover balance. Apple does not restore spent consumable credits.

Bugs and features

  • What you expected
  • iPhone model and iOS version

Feature ideas can go to the same address. Put “PLANIGATOR” in the subject.
