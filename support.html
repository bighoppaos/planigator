Support

Planigator is a truck trip planner. The website runs in any modern browser, including Android. The iPhone app adds turn-by-turn. We do not have user accounts. Website trips stay in this browser. A share link puts the trip in the URL so the other person can open the same plan. iPhone trips stay on the phone. Leftover iPhone credits follow the same Apple ID through iCloud.

Website

Open the planner on a phone, tablet, or computer. Type pickup and drop, or tap Use my location and allow this site when the browser asks. Calculate gives leave-by, 30-minute breaks, 10-hour rests, and leeway. Clearing site data deletes what you saved here.

Miles you type are yours. “Estimate truck miles” uses HERE truck routing for a 53-ft trailer at 80,000 lb, 13 ft 6 in high. Truck only — not car, bike, or walk. Hours-of-service times are planning aids, not legal advice and not a substitute for your ELD.

Billing and credits

The website does not sell credits. Credit packs are only in the iPhone app, sold through Apple. To refund or manage a purchase:

  • On iPhone: Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → Purchase History, or
  • reportaproblem.apple.com

Used credits stay used. Leftover credits follow this Apple ID through iCloud, so a new phone signed into the same account gets the leftover balance. Apple does not restore spent consumable credits.
