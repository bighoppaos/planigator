Support

Planigator is a truck trip planner that runs in your browser. Open the planner on a phone, tablet, or computer. Trips stay in this browser unless you sign in with Google, in which case saved trips are stored with that account. A share link puts the trip in the URL so the other person can open the same plan.

Planner

Type pickup and drop, or tap Start from my location and allow this site when the browser asks. Calculate asks HERE© for truck miles and drive hours, then Planigator gives leave-by, 30-minute breaks, 10-hour rests, and leeway. After Calculate, Share trip link puts that plan in the address bar so you can send it. Clearing site data deletes what you saved in this browser (account-saved trips stay with your sign-in).

HERE© truck routing is a 53-ft trailer at 80,000 lb, 13 ft 6 in high. Truck only — not car, bike, or walk. Hours-of-service times are planning aids, not legal advice and not a substitute for your ELD.

Billing and credits

Sign in with Google for 3 free credits, enough to try a trip. The first time you save a debit or credit card, that account gets 9 more credits, once. Removing that card and adding another card does not give another 9. Prepaid cards do not get that offer. We do not charge the saved card when the free credits run out. Each address lookup uses 1 credit. Each leg of Calculate uses 1 credit. A lookup that finds nothing is refunded. We never see the full card number.

Extra packs are 124 credits at $1.49.
