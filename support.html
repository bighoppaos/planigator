Support

Planigator is a truck trip planner that runs in your browser. Open the planner on a phone, tablet, or computer. Trips stay in this browser unless you sign in with Apple or Google, in which case saved trips are stored with that account. A share link puts the trip in the URL so the other person can open the same plan.

Planner

Type pickup and drop, or tap Use my location and allow this site when the browser asks. Calculate asks © HERE for truck miles and hours, then gives leave-by, 30-minute breaks, 10-hour rests, and leeway. Clearing site data deletes what you saved in this browser (account-saved trips stay with your sign-in).

© HERE truck routing is a 53-ft trailer at 80,000 lb, 13 ft 6 in high. Truck only — not car, bike, or walk. Hours-of-service times are planning aids, not legal advice and not a substitute for your ELD.

Billing and credits

Sign in with Apple or Google for 12 free credits, once per account. Each © HERE lookup uses 1 credit. A lookup that finds nothing is refunded.

When Stripe checkout is connected, extra packs are 124 credits at $1.49. If Stripe secrets are not set up yet, checkout may say it is not connected and packs are not on sale. We do not see your card number.
