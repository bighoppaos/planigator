Proposed privacy line

Our own links. If you open the site from an ad or a link we ran, we keep that link’s source, campaign, term, and content on this device and add one to a count of landings. A first Google sign-in adds one to a count of sign-ups for that same link. Those counts are totals only, with no name or email. Nothing is sent to Google, Meta, or any other ad company, and this site does not load their tags or pixels.

The line “We do not run ads” stays. It means no ads on the site.
