The website and the app

The website is the Planigator trip clock: pickup, drop, HERE truck routes, leave-by, rests, 30-minute breaks, and bedtime. The iPhone app also has Watch, Health, turn-by-turn, and Apple purchases. Hours-of-service numbers, ETAs, and traffic times are planning aids. They are not legal advice, not a substitute for your ELD, and not a guarantee you will arrive on time.

Miles you type on the website are yours. Address estimates use HERE truck routing for a 53-ft trailer at 80,000 lb, 13 ft 6 in high. They are not car, bike, or walk routes.

Credits

The website does not sell credits. On iPhone, HERE lookups cost credits. New installs start with a free batch of credits. Extra credits are one-time (consumable) In-App Purchases through Apple. Apple keeps a commission on those purchases. Used credits are not restored if you delete the app or tap Restore. Leftover credits follow the same Apple ID through iCloud.
