Build #622 10.3.26

Love's, Walmart, Cat scale, truck stop, and Swift terminal results show on the pins only. The numbered list of places at the top of the full-screen map is gone for good. Build #463 had removed it, but Build #537 brought it back by mistake, behind Search here and Clear.

Each place now reads name, miles ahead, and miles off the route, like Walmart Supercenter · 66.9 miles ahead · 0.8 miles off the route. The city and state are no longer shown. The other places are small numbered dots with no box, so the boxes no longer pile on top of each other or on your stop names. Only the place you chose has the full box with Add and recalculate. The first place is chosen to start. Tap another numbered dot to choose it, and the box moves there. If the box would run under Search here and Clear at the top, it sits under its dot instead. Search here and Clear stay between the side buttons, on the page map and in full screen. The list under the page map is still there. Searching, Add and recalculate, Recalculate, Turn zoom, and the colors are unchanged.

Build #621 10.3.26

Turn zoom no longer zooms in so far that you cannot tell which way the turn goes. Before, it fit the map only to the road between you and the next turn and put the turn near the top edge, so almost none of the road after the turn was on the screen. On an interchange ramp, one cloverleaf could fill the map. Now Turn zoom also keeps the road after the turn on the map, between the side buttons: at least a quarter mile of it, or half the distance from you to the turn when that is more, up to half a mile, and never past the stop you are driving to. When that road needs more room, the map zooms out around your dot and the turn dot moves down toward you. Your dot stays in its place on the screen as before.

Turn zoom now stops zooming in at street level. Before, close to a turn it could zoom in much further than the map normally goes. Now, past street level the map stays at street level, your dot stays in its place, and the turn dot sits on the real turn instead of at the top of the view. A road that loops back near a ramp, like a cloverleaf, no longer makes Turn zoom act as if you were already at the ramp while it is still most of a mile away. North lock, Follow me, Trip zoom, Left zoom, and Stop zoom are unchanged.

Build #620 10.3.26

The map's Recalculate no longer drops a stop you just added. Before, if you added a stop (for example a Pilot) in front of the stop you were heading to and pressed Recalculate, the new stop was marked as passed and left off the route, and the route went straight to the later stop. Now Recalculate routes from where you are to the new stop, then from the new stop on to the stop after it. Each of those legs is 1 credit, and if there are not enough credits nothing changes. If the new stop's address has not been looked up yet, Recalculate shows "Press lookup address on … and choose an address before pressing Recalculate." and changes nothing. Stops you really passed (done, or skipped on purpose) are still left behind as before. This works with navigation on or off.

End navigation no longer changes where the trip starts. Build #618 made End navigation switch the start to your location and mark the first stop passed. Now Choose where the trip starts, the start address, and your stops stay exactly as you left them. Instead, both Recalculate buttons route from where you are right now: the map's Recalculate, and the Calculate button once it says Recalculate. Each one asks the phone for a fresh location, and uses the last navigation spot if the phone does not answer. After either one, the trip starts from your location there; a start address you typed stays on its card, marked passed. The Calculate button still routes only the new legs when it says Recalculate from a stop, and the first Calculate of a new trip still starts where you chose. Stops marked done stay done.

The other stops stay green. The follow page, the stop pins, the routes, and Recalculate are unchanged.
