Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Planigator is a truck trip planner. The website runs in your browser. The iPhone app adds Watch, Health, and navigation. We do not sell your information. We do not run ads. We do not build a marketing profile of you.

What the website uses

This browser. Trips and HOS settings stay in this browser’s local storage. Clearing site data deletes them.

Credits. Calculate talks to a Planigator server that keeps your leftover credit count and sends the address or coordinates to HERE. 12 free credits are granted once after you sign in with Apple or Google.

Share links. If you tap Share trip link, the trip is packed into the page URL. Anyone with that link can open the same plan. We do not host the link.

Location. Only if you tap Use my location. The browser asks first. That point is used as the start of a HERE truck route when you Calculate.

HERE. When you Calculate, the server sends those addresses or coordinates to HERE Technologies for a truck-legal route (53-ft trailer, 80,000 lb, 13 ft 6 in). Truck only — not car, bike, or walk. HERE’s policy: legal.here.com/privacy

Payments. Website credit packs are charged by Stripe. We do not see your card number. Stripe’s policy: stripe.com/privacy

What the app uses on your phone

Location. The app asks for your location so it can start a route from where you are, follow you while you drive, and estimate drive time. Location stays on the phone except when a route or place lookup needs it.

Purchases. If you buy credits, Apple takes the payment. The app only learns that the purchase succeeded and how many credits to add. We do not see your card number. Apple’s cut is Apple’s — we never receive your payment details.

iCloud. The leftover credit balance syncs with Apple iCloud on the same Apple ID so it can follow you to a new phone. We do not read your other iCloud files.

HERE. Truck-legal routes and some lookups are calculated by HERE Technologies. The app sends the coordinates or search text for that request to HERE with our API key. HERE’s policy: legal.here.com/privacy

Microphone and camera. On iPhone, only if you record a memo, plus Photos and on-device speech if you choose those.

Audio and PDFs. Files you add stay on your phone.

What we do not do

We do not store location history. Sign-in stores a credit balance and the trips you save. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Credits

The website gives 12 free credits once after Apple or Google sign-in. Each HERE lookup uses 1. Extra website packs are 124 credits at 3× a HERE call, sold through Stripe. On iPhone, leftover credits follow the same Apple ID through iCloud.
