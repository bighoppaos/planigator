Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Planigator is a truck trip planner that runs in your browser. We do not sell your information. We do not run ads. We do not build a marketing profile of you.

What this website uses

This browser. Trips and HOS settings stay in this browser’s local storage unless you are signed in. Clearing site data deletes what is saved here.

Sign-in. Google sign-in gives 40 free credits and stores the trips you save with that account on a Planigator server. It also stores the name and email from that Google account. An hour with no taps signs that account out. The owner account can open a private list of those names, emails, credits used, credits left, whether a card is saved, and who used the site in the last hour.

Credits. Address lookup and Calculate talk to a Planigator server that keeps your leftover credit count and sends the address or coordinates to HERE©. The first saved debit or credit card adds 40 credits, once per account. You can remove that saved card. A later card on that account does not add another 40. We store the card brand, last four digits, and Stripe’s card fingerprint. We never see the full card number. We do not charge the saved card when the free credits run out. Each address lookup uses 1 credit. Each leg of a route uses 1 credit. A lookup that finds nothing is refunded.

Call log. Each address lookup and truck route is saved with the time, the address or the two points, whether HERE© answered, and which browser session sent it. That is so a HERE bill can be matched to a person. The log does not include the HERE key.

Share links. After Calculate, Share trip link packs the trip into the page URL. Anyone with that link can open the same plan. We do not host the link.

Location. Only if you tap Start from my location, or Start navigation. The browser asks first. A point from Start from my location is used as the start of a HERE© truck route when you Calculate. During navigation, the moving position stays on this phone. It is not saved and it is not sent to Planigator.

Compass and motion. Start navigation asks the phone for compass and motion so the map can turn with the truck. The browser asks first. That heading stays on this phone. It is not saved and it is not sent to Planigator.

HERE©. When you Calculate, the server sends those addresses or coordinates to HERE Technologies for a truck-legal route (53-ft trailer, 80,000 lb, 13 ft 6 in). Truck only — not car, bike, or walk. HERE’s policy: legal.here.com/privacy

Payments. Stripe saves the card and charges credit packs. We do not see the full card number. Stripe’s policy: stripe.com/privacy

What we do not do

We do not store location history, compass headings, or motion readings. Sign-in stores a credit balance and the trips you save to your account. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Credits

The website gives 40 free credits after Google sign-in, and 40 more the first time that account saves a debit or credit card. Another card later does not add 40 again. Prepaid cards do not get that offer. We do not charge the saved card when the free credits run out. Each address lookup uses 1 credit. Each route leg uses 1. Extra packs are 124 credits at $1.49.
