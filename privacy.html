Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Planigator is a truck trip planner that runs in your browser. We do not sell your information. We do not run ads. We do not build a marketing profile of you.

What this website uses

This browser. Trips and HOS settings stay in this browser’s local storage unless you are signed in. Clearing site data deletes what is saved here.

Sign-in. Google sign-in stores the trips you save with that account on a Planigator server. It does not grant credits.

Credits. Calculate talks to a Planigator server that keeps your leftover credit count and sends the address or coordinates to HERE©. 12 free credits are granted once after Stripe saves a debit or credit card that has not been used for this offer. We store the card brand, last four digits, and Stripe’s card fingerprint. We never see the full card number. Each HERE© lookup uses 1 credit. A lookup that finds nothing is refunded.

Call log. Each address lookup and truck route is saved with the time, the address or the two points, whether HERE© answered, and which browser session sent it. That is so a HERE bill can be matched to a person. The log does not include the HERE key.

Share links. If you tap Share trip link, the trip is packed into the page URL. Anyone with that link can open the same plan. We do not host the link.

Location. Only if you tap Use my location. The browser asks first. That point is used as the start of a HERE© truck route when you Calculate.

HERE©. When you Calculate, the server sends those addresses or coordinates to HERE Technologies for a truck-legal route (53-ft trailer, 80,000 lb, 13 ft 6 in). Truck only — not car, bike, or walk. HERE’s policy: legal.here.com/privacy

Payments. Stripe saves the card and charges credit packs. We do not see the full card number. Until Stripe finishes verifying this account, the card form and packs stay off. Stripe’s policy: stripe.com/privacy

What we do not do

We do not store location history. Sign-in stores a credit balance and the trips you save to your account. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Credits

The website gives 12 free credits once per debit or credit card saved with Stripe. Prepaid cards do not get that offer. Each HERE© lookup uses 1. Extra packs are 124 credits at $1.49 once Stripe verification is finished.
